THE DEPENDENCY CHAIN
4 Why the current PASS cannot stand
Forecast validation is hierarchical: later evidence is only as credible as earlier evidence. A score can be numerically proper
while being scientifically meaningless if the outcome attached to the race is wrong. A sealed rebuild can perfectly reproduce
an invalid data-generating pipeline. The current system’s strongest controls operate downstream of the broken truth root.

CURRENT BREAK AT THE ROOT
The first box is generated from internal constants and contains incorrect contests/results.
Every later score, stack weight, calibration claim, and release decision inherits that defect.
A valid forecast is a chain of evidence. Reproducibility downstream cannot repair invalid truth upstream.

Circular chamber reconciliation
 TAUTOLOGY EMBEDDED IN THE TRUTH GATE
The archive computes held Democratic seats as held_D = target_post_D − contested_D_wins, then
validates held_D + contested_D_wins = target_post_D. The second equality follows algebraically
from the first; it is not independent evidence that the seat accounting is correct.
The contest universe has the same problem. contested_contests(year) produces the records written to the official race
store, and the chamber validator calls that same function to produce its expected IDs. Missing elections encoded in neither
place are invisible to the comparison. This is why 33-versus-33 passes even when the official ballot contained 35 Senate
contests.
Control-design conclusion
A release gate must compare two independently derived objects. Here, the expected universe, held-seat totals, and
post-election target are all co-generated from CYCLE_META/CERTIFIED_MARGINS. The correct replacement is an
immutable official event ledger ingested from external source files, plus a validator whose expectations are coded
separately and spot-checked against a second source.

Three design defects
 • Selection on the outcome of being polled. The denominator is built only from contests that received at least one
 non-hypothetical poll within the cycle. A race with zero polls disappears rather than registering zero coverage.
• Self-confirming nominee identity. Nominees are inferred from late poll matchups and those inferred nominees are then
 used to check the same poll stream. A consistently wrong matchup can validate itself.
• Lenient lead thresholds. Minimum shares are 40% at 90 days, 55% at 60 days, and 70% at 30/7 days. Those may be
 reasonable data-availability warnings, but they cannot support a ‘complete-cycle’ claim without an explicit model-based
unpolled-race policy.
Blueprint-aligned correction
Define the ballot denominator from an independent official event ledger. Define general-election nominees from official
candidate lists and election phase/availability records. Report coverage over (a) every official contest, (b) competitive
contests under a predeclared, vintaged competitiveness rule, and (c) the poll-observed subset. Do not silently equate (c)
with the universe.

STRONG ARCHITECTURE; PRODUCTION REMAINS STATIC
9 Statistical core
The core architecture now resembles the best traditions in modern election forecasting. Polls observe a latent race state,
mode and population effects are hierarchical in PyMC, heavy-tailed shocks are used, and the simulator shares national and
similarity-driven uncertainty across contests. The project also labels the production fit honestly: static Election-Day latent,
with a weekly random-walk challenger.
Conceptual model
 MEASUREMENT AND EVOLUTION
Poll measurement: yᵢ = θᵣ,  + houseᵢ + modeᵢ + populationᵢ + εᵢ. Latent path: θᵣ,  = fundamentalsᵣ +
national  + localᵣ, . Election Day: marginᵣ = θᵣ,T + terminal-national + terminal-race + similarity
shock.
What is good
 • Student-t national, regional/local, future, and terminal terms reduce thin-tail overconfidence.
 • Future movement contracts with days to Election Day while terminal error remains distinct—an important
 Silver/Morris-style separation.
• Dynamic fit exposes weekly national and race paths and maps each poll to its poll week.
 • Similarity post-draw shocks create coherent maps instead of independent race noise.
 • Static and dynamic paths have explicit method/latent_path labels, preventing the prior identity ambiguity.
 
What is not yet earned
The packaged static-versus-dynamic artifact covers only 2022 with 100 draws. Dynamic CRPS is worse than static at 90,
60, and 30 days, better at 7 days, and worse by +0.642 on average (negative would favor dynamic). Keeping static
production is therefore sensible, but neither the dynamic hyperparameters nor its added complexity are validated. Because
the outcome ledger is also invalid, even this comparison must be rerun after P0 repair.

Correlated uncertainty
The decomposition is well chosen and aligns with Economist/Morris correlated state error, Silver’s

GOOD TIME-FREEZING; OVERSTATED NESTING
11 Backtesting, leakage, and ensemble construction
What the replay gets right
 • As-of snapshots are built before outcome scoring.
 • Predictions are frozen before the result table is read.
 • Component identities and failures are retained rather than silently relabeled.
 • Scores include continuous margin metrics and chamber summaries rather than winner accuracy alone.
 
Why ‘nested component LOO’ is too strong a label
The driver loops over years, fits each component to that year’s as-of snapshot, freezes it, and then reveals truth. That is a
valid historical-date replay structure. But the function does not, for every trainable component, perform an inner
year-grouped hyperparameter selection on the other cycles and refit a fold-specific learner. In particular,
fit_ridge_fundamentals does not estimate ridge coefficients; it calls the shared prior-based fundamentals mean. The replay’s
generic-ballot value is also inferred from state Senate polls minus prior lean, so the ‘fundamentals-only’ challenger is not
independent of the race-poll archive.
Current weighting rule
 SCORE SOFTMAX IS NOT PREDICTIVE STACKING
The implemented rule is approximately w    exp(−mean_CRPS  / T). Predictive stacking instead
chooses simplex weights that optimize a proper score of the combined race-level OOF predictive
distributions. Aggregate-score softmax cannot learn complementarity, correlated errors, or which
model rescues another model’s failures.

Overall comparative judgment
The codebase is now closer to leading models in architecture than its current public-readiness score suggests. Its largest
gap is not an exotic statistical technique. Top forecasters differ on priors, poll adjustments, and correlation strength, but all
meaningful comparisons presuppose a correct contest universe and observed outcomes. Electionmodel26 should pause
feature expansion until it can prove that foundation.
DO NOT ENSEMBLE PUBLIC PROBABILITIES FROM PEERS
Use peer models as methodological comparators and external calibration checks, not as probability
ingredients. Most models share polls and correlated information; averaging final probabilities would
double-count evidence and obscure responsibility for errors.

A STRONG PROTOTYPE WITH A REPAIRABLE FOUNDATION PROBLEM
18 Final assessment
Electionmodel26 v0.9.16 is not the same prototype that was previously reviewed. It has absorbed many of the blueprint’s
strongest ideas and implemented them with unusual speed: hierarchical polling, explicit uncertainty layers, dynamic
challengers, model identity controls, proper-score artifacts, shadow releases, signing, and rebuild checks. That progress is
real.
The publication claim is nevertheless unsound. Three omitted groups of special elections, at least two official result canaries
with reversed winners, solved held-seat counts, fixture-backed live fundamentals, and two clean safety-test failures are not
minor calibration issues. They strike the outcome and evidence roots from which every score and gate is derived.
The right response is a disciplined reset of evidence—not a rewrite of the whole model. Preserve the architecture, the
static/dynamic separation, the uncertainty decomposition, and the operational sealing. Replace the truth ledger, broaden
fail-closed eligibility, rebuild the historical feature matrix, and only then rerun nested validation and ensemble optimization. If
those steps are completed, this codebase could become a serious Senate forecasting system.
FINAL VERDICT
Research use: CONTINUE WITH EXPLICIT NON-PUBLICATION LABEL. Public live probabilities:
HOLD. Re-audit: after independent truth + all-domain evidence gates are demonstrably closed.

Decision precedence
The audit does not average away foundational defects. A single verified P0 failure overrides strong architecture, many
passing tests, or a high weighted score. ‘Reproducible’ means the computation can be repeated; ‘valid’ means the
computation estimates the intended quantity from appropriate evidence. Both are required for publication.
