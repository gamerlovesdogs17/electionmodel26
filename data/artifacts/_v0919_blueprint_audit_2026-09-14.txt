1. Executive decision
The latest iteration is a serious improvement, but it is not yet a top-tier public forecasting product. It is best described
as a strong research engineering platform with an invalid release-evidence layer. The project now resembles
leading systems in its statistical vocabulary and many of its implementation patterns. Its remaining weaknesses are
more basic and more consequential: what counts as truth, what counts as a real input, and whether the backtest
genuinely recreates what could have been known at the time.
BOTTOM LINE
Do not chase peer-model surface similarity. Keep the blueprint's validation-first direction, but revise it
to demand semantic source verification, rolling-origin historical evaluation, version-coherent external
review, and first-class multi-candidate election truth. The next work should be data and validation, not
another model layer.

The audit evaluates the latest supplied ZIP against the original blueprint embedded in the project, the actual source and machine
artifacts, an executed test run, and documented methods from leading forecast traditions. Attached documents were treated as evidence
of intent or prior claims, not as instructions. The review did not alter the model and does not reproduce its current candidate probabilities.

counts are reconstructed. The audit distinguishes an implementation defect from a debatable modeling choice and from a peer
difference that the blueprint does not require.

The source URLs do not repair the values
Every row in a cycle shares one generic URL. The 2024 URL is a federal-candidate list, not a certified Senate results file. A source link

separate; the execution path shows they are not independent. This repairs the earlier circular equation at runtime but preserves
common-source circularity at build time.

Why the economic timestamp is leakage
FRED's default real-time period describes what is known today; ALFRED exists to retrieve what was known on a historical date.[8] The
code sets realtime_start = observation_date and available_at = observation_date when reading a public CSV.
Economic observations are normally released after their reference period and later revised. This transformation therefore grants
historical backtests data too early and at revised values.
Why the finance fallback is worse than missing
OpenFEC provides candidate and committee filings, coverage periods, amendment chains, and nightly-updated data.[9] A missing or
rate-limited feed should disable the finance component or preserve the last sealed valid snapshot. Generating finance shares from
partisan lean creates circular confirmation: the model appears to use a separate feature while actually reusing its prior.

6.4 P0 - Historical validation is not information-time pure
The replay freezes predictions before reading the target year's result table, which is good. However, the ridge challenger then creates a
historical training matrix from every result cycle except the snapshot's election ID. For a 2018 holdout, that admits 2020, 2022, and 2024
outcomes. This is leave-one-cycle-out in name, but not a historical as-of forecast.
Required replacement
Use rolling-origin replay as the primary historical claim: for each target cycle, fit only on elections,
source versions, pollster ratings, economic releases, and configuration choices available before that
target date. Symmetric leave-one-cycle-out can remain a secondary data-efficiency sensitivity analysis,
clearly labeled as non-prospective.
Other fold-purity defects
●
 The ridge call in the nested driver does not pass an explicitly fold-built training matrix; it relies on a global warehouse fallback.
●
 The 'fundamentals-only' generic ballot is inferred from state Senate polls minus prior lean, so it is not independent of the race-poll
archive.
●
 The static/dynamic comparison still covers only 2022 and uses 80 draws per fit. Keeping static production is sensible given those
results, but the comparison is not a multi-cycle selection gate.
6.5 P1 - 'Predictive mixture CRPS' is still stacking of means
The stacker now stores race-level out-of-fold means, a meaningful advance. Its objective forms pred = M w and evaluates a Normal
distribution centered on that weighted mean with a fixed six-point standard deviation. It does not combine component predictive
distributions. Production, by contrast, selects one component's full joint draw for each simulation. Training and deployment therefore
optimize different distributions.
Implemented objective
Current approximation
w* = arg min_w mean CRPS[ Normal(Mw, 6), y ], with w on the simplex.
Blueprint target: w* = arg min_w mean S[ sum_m w_m F_m, y ], using fold-pure predictive draws F_m
and the same mixture mechanism deployed in production.
The current production weights are approximately 0.409 last-election swing, 0.392 ridge fundamentals, and 0.199 state-space; PyMC
receives zero mass. That is not inherently a defect - a blueprint-first system should let simple models win. It becomes unusable evidence
because the ridge is future-contaminated, the outcome ledger is reconstructed, and the weight optimizer ignores component variance
and covariance. Yao et al. explicitly distinguish stacking predictive distributions from stacking means.[12]

12. What not to do next
●
 Do not add another machine-learning learner, more simulations, or additional overlays before fixing truth and vintages.
●
 Do not force positive PyMC weight or tune toward a preferred public probability. Zero weight is a diagnostic, not a reputational
problem.
●
 Do not convert missing live finance into a modeled estimate and then call it observed finance. Disable the feature or propagate
missingness uncertainty.
●
 Do not use a broad FEC or 538 URL as provenance for hand-entered numbers. Provenance must identify the exact object and row
transformation.
●
 Do not call symmetric leave-one-cycle-out 'historical as-of' when later cycles train earlier folds.
●
 Do not treat all-PASS JSON as independent clearance when the same code and constants produce both the evidence and the
expectations.
●
 Do not copy FLIPR, DDHQ, VoteHub, or Economist parameters. Recreate their useful hypotheses inside this model's own honest
validation harness.

Audit conclusion
Electionmodel26 has reached the point where additional sophistication is not the limiting factor. Its best path toward
a top-tier model is to make the evidence and validation system as rigorous as the probabilistic architecture: exact
truth, real historical vintages, fold-pure training, deployed-distribution scoring, adversarial gates, and independent
version-coherent publication review.
